WE CREATE SPACES WITH PURPOSE.

AIS Concepts Ltd is an architecture and built-environment practice focused on creating thoughtful, functional and distinctive spaces through architecture, interior design, planning and project management.

VIEW OUR PROJECTS START A PROJECT

Architecture is more than creating buildings. It is about shaping the way people experience space.

AIS Concepts Ltd is a multidisciplinary architecture and design practice based in Kenya. We work across residential, commercial, hospitality, and institutional projects, bringing together architectural design, interior design, planning, and project management expertise.

Our approach is rooted in the belief that great spaces emerge from understanding context, function, and human experience. We work closely with clients to develop solutions that are both practical and aspirational, creating environments that serve their intended purpose while leaving room for growth and adaptation.
